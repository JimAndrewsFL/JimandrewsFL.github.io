About

Welcome. I am a Professor at the University of South Florida, School of Information. My research intersects with information science and health informatics, and I collaborate with a range of faculty and other partners.

Recently, we have been developing a student health informatics research lab with students from different programs across USF. We are continually seeking new collaborators (especially faculty mentors) to develop new research projects. I encourage you to reach out if you have shared interests or ideas for collaborating.

Research Interests

  • Library and information science
  • Health informatics
  • Health terminologies and standards
  • Health information behaviors
  • Scholarly communication and bibliometrics
